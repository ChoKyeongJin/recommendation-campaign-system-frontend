The OGG files in piano-samples/ are unmodified copies of ten notes from the
package's softly played velocity-3 layer. The distributor's sample information
is preserved in sample-source-readme.txt.

For this video, a new 45-second piano arrangement was sequenced from these
individual notes. Playback includes pitch interpolation, note envelopes, stereo
balance, light room reflections, equalization, and loudness adjustment. This is
not an existing recorded musical performance or a claim of the sample author's
endorsement. No percussion or continuous oscillator pad is used.
